Become a tester

Six Android apps are in closed testing on Google Play. Testing is free and takes a couple of minutes.

How it works

  1. Send the Gmail address you use on your phone to the developer, so it can be added to the tester list. The links below only work once that is done.
  2. On your Android phone, signed in to that Google account, open a link below and tap Become a tester.
  3. Follow the link to Google Play and install the app.
  4. Keep it installed for at least 14 days and open it now and then. That is what Google counts.
